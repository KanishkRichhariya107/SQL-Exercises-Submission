(Scan type)-  Seq Scan, Index Scan, Index Only Scan
(on table)- the table that is being scanned
(cost=a..b)- a is startup cost, b is total cost
(rows) - Estimated number of rows returned by that node
(filter)- condition that is applied to the rows
